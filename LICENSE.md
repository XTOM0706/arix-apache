## 常见问题 / FAQ

**能不能收费 / 挂捐赠？** 能。AGPL 第 4 条明文允许对分发收取任意价格，也允许
有偿提供支持。捐赠更不受约束。原作者与二次开发者都可以挂捐赠——只要遵守
AGPL（公开源码、保留署名）。

**Can I charge money / accept donations?** Yes. AGPL section 4 expressly permits
charging any price for conveying copies, and offering paid support. Donations are
not restricted at all. Both the original author and downstream developers may
accept donations — provided they comply with the AGPL (publish source, preserve
attribution).

**我改了自己用，要开源吗？** 不用。AGPL 只在你**分发**或**对外提供网络服务**时
才要求提供源码。自己用随便改。

**Do I have to publish source if I only modify it for myself?** No. The AGPL only
requires source when you **convey** the work or **offer it as a network service**.
Private modification is unrestricted.

**为什么是 AGPL 而不是 GPL？** 为了堵上「把改版当网络服务跑、不分发 App 就不必
放源码」这个洞。Arix 有云端市场/API 相关能力，AGPL 才管得住。

**Why AGPL rather than GPL?** To close the loophole where someone runs a modified
version as a network service without distributing the app, and thus would not have
to release source under plain GPL.
